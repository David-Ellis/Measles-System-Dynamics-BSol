
==================================================
PART 0 – HEADER AND EXPLANATION
==================================================

This document explains how the chronological extraction of the workshop transcripts has been produced. It applies to Parts 1–3 and the combined “chronological_extraction_FULL.txt”.

--------------------------------------------
1. Purpose
--------------------------------------------
The purpose of the extraction is to present all substantive content from both Microsoft Teams transcript files in clean, rewritten bullet‑point form, sorted strictly by the agenda headings supplied by the user. This ensures that the final files can be used for analysis, modelling inputs, and documentation while remaining anonymised and structured.

--------------------------------------------
2. Source Material
--------------------------------------------
The extraction is based solely on:
• “Workshop transcript part 1.docx”
• “Workshop transcript part 2.docx”

Both files were provided by the user. No additional sources or interpretations beyond the transcripts were used.

--------------------------------------------
3. Anonymisation Rules
--------------------------------------------
• No names, role titles, or identifiable details of speakers are included.
• All contributions are rewritten in the third person, without attribution.
• Any content that would reveal an individual’s identity has been removed or generalised.

--------------------------------------------
4. Formatting Rules
--------------------------------------------
The user selected:
• Bullet‑point format (Option B).
• Unclear or ambiguous transcript lines are included as separate bullet points (Option 1).
• No timestamps are included (other than agenda headings).
• No names or speaker markers are included.
• Each bullet point is a clean rewritten sentence or group of related sentences.
• No citations, no external content, no interpretation beyond clarity.

--------------------------------------------
5. Handling of Unclear, Garbled, or Partial Transcript Lines
--------------------------------------------
• Any content that was incomplete, not fully audible, or presented as fragmented text is included as a separate bullet point beginning with “[UNCLEAR]”.
• If content was partially understandable, the unclear portion is marked as “[UNCLEAR]” within the bullet point.

--------------------------------------------
6. Agenda Structure Used
--------------------------------------------
The extraction is organised under the agenda headings provided by the user, in the following order:

Introduction
10:00 Arrival
10:10 Welcome and project overview
10:20 Local epidemiology update (UKHSA)
10:30 Summary of key evidence on vaccination barriers
11:00 Brief introduction to system dynamics modelling
11:10 System dynamics interactive example (group task)
11:30 Break

Model Building
11:45 Base model introduction
11:55 Identify factors that influence key flows in the system (group task)
12:20 Discussion
12:30 Vote on most influential factors
12:45 Lunch Break

Interventions
13:30 Identify problematic flows in the system
13:40 Identify potential interventions to improve system behaviour (group task)
14:10 Discussion
14:30 Rank proposed interventions using an anonymous online vote
15:00 Finish

--------------------------------------------
7. Ordering Rules
--------------------------------------------
• All content is presented chronologically as it appeared in the transcripts.
• When part of a discussion obviously connects to a specific agenda item, it is placed there even if the transcript timing overlaps slightly.
• All content is preserved except non‑substantive filler such as repeated phrases (“yeah yeah yeah”), unless they were part of an unclear line, in which case they are replaced by “[UNCLEAR]”.

--------------------------------------------
8. Files Produced
--------------------------------------------
The full extraction set includes:

Part 0 – Header/Explanation  
Part 1 – Introduction section  
Part 2 – Model Building section  
Part 3 – Interventions section  
chronological_extraction_FULL.txt – Complete combined extraction

--------------------------------------------
9. Scope and Limitations
--------------------------------------------
• The content is a cleaned, structured rewrite of the transcripts, not a verbatim reproduction.
• No interpretive conclusions are added.
• Content is only as accurate as the clarity of the transcripts provided.
• Unclear or ambiguous content is included but flagged.
• Agenda alignment is based on transcript sequencing and topic changes.

--------------------------------------------
10. Intended Use
--------------------------------------------
This extraction is designed for:
• System dynamics modelling preparation
• Thematic synthesis
• Workshop evaluation
• Dissertation documentation
• Organisational review and planning

End of Part 0.
